# 🌟 Crea8or — Executive Product Brief & Overview

> **The All-In-One Business Operating System (BOS) for Modern Creative Professionals & Production Studios.**

---

## 📌 1. Executive Summary

**Crea8or** is an enterprise-grade SaaS platform built specifically for photographers, videographers, filmmakers, wedding specialists, digital creators, and creative agencies in Africa and emerging markets.

Today, creative professionals lose **15+ hours weekly** juggling fragmented software: WhatsApp chats for inquiries, Excel for quotes, Google Drive for unbranded file delivery, manual Paystack links for deposits, and scattered notes for shoot call sheets. Furthermore, global alternatives (HoneyBook, Dubsado, Pixieset, Frame.io) charge in USD, ignore local African payment rails, and do not integrate seamlessly with local creative workflows.

**Crea8or consolidates the entire creative business lifecycle into a single high-end dark platform:**
From first inquiry ➔ interactive proposal ➔ digital contract & Paystack deposit ➔ shoot call sheet ➔ branded 4K photo delivery & frame-accurate video review ➔ true net profit tracking.

---

## 🎯 2. The Target Audience

1. **Photographers**: Wedding, fashion, portrait, corporate, and event photographers needing client proofing, album favoriting, and high-speed 4K delivery.
2. **Filmmakers & Videographers**: Commercial directors and video creators requiring frame-accurate client feedback and version approvals (Frame.io replacement).
3. **Wedding & Event Specialists**: High-ticket event teams requiring detailed questionnaires, milestone deposits, and complex call sheets.
4. **Creative Agencies & Production Studios**: Multi-crew teams managing client directories, team RBAC permissions, and project expense P&L margins.
5. **Digital Content Creators**: Creators managing brand sponsorships, deliverables checklists, and inbound press inquiries.

### 1. 🌐 Public Showroom & 24/7 Booking Engine (`/p/[slug]` & `/b/[slug]`)
- **Creator Showroom**: A stunning, branded portfolio page with bio, work samples, reviews, and a 1-click booking CTA.
- **Self-Serve Booking Engine**: Clients pick service packages, add customizable deliverables (e.g. drone coverage, extra retouched photos), and view instant pricing with deposit milestones.

### 2. 📋 Leads Kanban Pipeline & CRM (`/leads` & `/clients`)
- Visual deal stages (*New Inquiry ➔ Contacted ➔ Quote Sent ➔ Negotiating ➔ Booked*).
- Complete client directory with lifetime booking history, outstanding invoices, and communication logs.

### 3. 💸 Dynamic Proposals & Paystack Invoicing (`/quotes` & `/invoices`)
- **1-Click Proposal Generator**: Calculates subtotal, tax rate, and discounts automatically.
- **Paystack Payment Rails**: Built-in payment gateway supporting **₦ NGN, GH₵ GHS, KSh KES, R ZAR, $ USD, £ GBP** with instant HMAC-verified webhook reconciliation.
- **Digital Contracts**: E-signature with IP address logging and client timestamp verification.

### 4. 🎬 Production Operations & Shoot Call Sheets (`/projects/[id]`)
- **Printable / Digital Call Sheets**: Crew call times, location GPS coordinates, weather forecasts, gear checklists, and emergency contacts.
- **Project Workspaces**: Centralizes all files, tasks, client questionnaires, and deliverables in one tabbed view.

### 5. 📸 Branded 4K Photo Galleries (`/galleries` & `/g/[slug]`)
- **Pixieset Alternative**: Delivers photos in a luxury dark masonry grid.
- **Camera EXIF Inspection**: Displays camera body, lens, aperture, shutter speed, and ISO on hover.
- **Client Album Proofing**: Clients "Heart" their favorite photos to curate final photo albums.
- **1-Click 4K ZIP Downloads**: High-speed cloud downloads for full photo collections.

### 6. 🎥 Frame-Accurate Video Review & Approval Player (`/review/[id]`)
- **Frame.io Alternative for Filmmakers**: 
- Scrubber timeline with real-time timecode tracking (`01:42 / 03:30`) and visual comment marker pins.
- Timestamped feedback thread where clicking any timecode jumps the video directly to that exact frame.
- 1-Click client version approval (*"Approve Cut V2"*).

### 7. 📊 Expense Logging & True Net Profit P&L (`/expenses`)
- Tracks project costs across **Crew Fees, Gear Rentals, Transportation, Studio Rentals, and Post-Production**.
- Computes real-time **Net Profit Margin %** so creators know their actual take-home earnings on every shoot.

### 8. 🛡️ Enterprise Security & Multi-Tenancy (`/settings/security`)
- Role-Based Access Control (Owner, Admin, Member, Crew).
- Two-Factor Authentication (2FA TOTP QR codes for Google Authenticator/1Password).
- Active logged-in device sessions with remote 1-click session revocation.

## 🛠️ 6. Technology Highlights

- **Frontend**: Next.js 15 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind CSS.
- **Backend**: Bun + Hono v4 REST API (Sub-millisecond latency).
- **Database**: PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM (Fully type-safe multi-tenant isolation).
- **Authentication**: Better Auth with 2FA TOTP & Organization plugins.
- **Payments**: Paystack API with HMAC-SHA512 webhook security.
