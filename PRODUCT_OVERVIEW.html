Executive Product Overview
Version 1.0 • Confidential • 2026

The Operating System for Creative Professionals

Crea8or is an enterprise-grade Business Operating System (BOS) consolidating CRM, automated proposals, Paystack payment rails, shoot call sheets, branded 4K photo proofing, and frame-accurate video review into one high-end dark platform.

◆ 1. The Core Problem & Market Opportunity
The Current Frustration (Old Way)
• Creators juggle 5+ disconnected tools (HoneyBook, Pixieset, Frame.io, Excel, WhatsApp).
• Forced to pay expensive USD subscriptions with international cards.
• Delivery via unbranded Google Drive links damages luxury client perception.
• Manual bank transfers cause friction, delayed deposits, and quote drop-offs.
The Crea8or Solution (The BOS)
• 1 Unified Platform handling the full lifecycle from lead to delivery.
• African payment rails first (₦ NGN, GHS, KES, USD) via native Paystack.
• Luxury 4K client portals commanding 3x higher client rates.
• Instant digital proposal signing & deposits closing deals in <15 mins.
◆ 2. Complete Product Architecture
⚡ 24/7 Booking Engine
Customizable package picker with real-time add-on calculations and automatic Paystack deposit quotes.
📋 Leads Kanban CRM
Visual deal pipeline tracking inquiries from initial contact through quote sent, negotiation, and booking.
💸 Dynamic Proposals
1-click proposals with dynamic tax/discount math, digital signatures, and automated invoice reconciliation.
🎬 Shoot Call Sheets
Printable & digital production call sheets with GPS coordinates, crew times, gear checklists, and weather info.
📸 4K Photo Galleries
Pixieset alternative with dark luxury masonry layouts, camera EXIF inspection, and album favoriting.
🎥 Video Review Player
Frame.io alternative with timecode markers, timestamped feedback jumping, and 1-click cut approvals.
